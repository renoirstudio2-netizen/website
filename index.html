Frequently asked questions

What exactly does Renoir do?

We help B2B companies build structured sales systems that reliably generate qualified leads and sales opportunities, making it a repeatable process every month.

That means clear processes, automation where it matters, and a focus on metrics that actually move the needle—no fluff.

How are you different from a marketing agency or lead gen service?

We don’t “bring leads” and leave the rest to chance. We build workflows that stays with you forever.

Renoir builds the full sales engine—from targeting and messaging to outreach and pipeline development—so your team isn’t guessing, and growth isn’t random.

Is this right for early-stage companies?

We only work with startups starting from Seed Stage.
Or bootstrapped startups if they're past $ 100K ARR.

If you’re still figuring out what to sell or don’t have the capacity to follow up with leads, we’re not the right fit, yet.

What kind of companies do you work with?

We work with B2B companies offering high-ticket products or services, typically in tech, SaaS, consulting, or specialized services.

If your clients are other businesses and your sales cycles aren’t instant, that’s our lane.

How long until we see results?

Assuming your offer is solid and your team is responsive, you’ll typically start seeing qualified opportunities within the first 7–20 days.

But this isn’t a “magic button”—it’s a system, and it builds over time.

Can you guarantee a number of leads or sales?

No. And anyone who does is lying or outsourcing low-quality appointments.

We guarantee a proven process, an upgrade in your current infrastructure, transparency in what’s working, and continuous optimization, but precise outcomes depend on many variables, including your team’s ability.

What does working with Renoir look like?

We analyze your current sales setup, design or refine your outbound and inbound engines, implement systems, and often train or augment your team.

It’s collaborative, hands-on, and focused on building something that keeps delivering, even after we’re done.

You can consider us "your co-founders without equity".
